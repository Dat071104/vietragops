# Gate 10 Adversarial Self-Review

Date: 2026-09-11

This review is part of the paper package. It is a hostile review of the
measurement claim, not a claim of independent peer review.

## Is the criterion novel, or is it only test-set validity restated?

It is adjacent to test-set validity, not a replacement for that literature. The
specific contribution is operational: declare information rights, audit each
ground-truth target for derivability of both values and construction steps, and
emit typed, reproducible unreachable-oracle statuses. The paper does not claim a
new theory of validity or a universal semantic-equivalence test.

## Does the real-version sample support the synthetic-versus-real claim?

No broad claim is supported. The hand-verified real MCP/API version pairs are a
purposive negative control. Their zero unreachable result shows that the auditor
does not mark every evolution as defective; it does not estimate real-world
prevalence or establish external validation.

## Is the self-authored sandbox fatal to the unreachability measurement?

It limits generality and is disclosed as a central limitation. It does not erase
the local measurement: the defect is independently reproduced, typed, and
traceable to committed artifacts. The defensible claim is that benchmark authors
can apply the criterion, not that every external benchmark has the same defect.

## Is the cancelled campaign a null result dressed up as a power result?

No. No repaired-lane comparative campaign ran. The power analysis is a design
decision showing that the retained surface could not resolve the preregistered
effect; it is not evidence that methods are equivalent and not a failed method
result.

## What stops this from being only a project report?

The research-facing objects are the formal reachability criterion, the reusable
offline auditor, the independent reproduction of the load-bearing findings, the
typed failure classes, the information-rights comparison, and the item-level
evidence ledger. Deployment details are explicitly bounded to a short case
study and are not presented as scientific contributions.

## Claim-language audit

- ``show'' is used only for the measured power consequence, and the wording is
  bounded to the frozen retained surface.
- ``observe'', ``measure'', and ``report'' are used for the empirical findings.
- No sentence claims SOTA, first benchmark, external validation, peer review,
  or generalization beyond the measured scope.
